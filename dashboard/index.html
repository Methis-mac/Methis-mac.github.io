Henry's Dashboard

A personal dashboard that runs privately on its owner's own computer.

Henry's Dashboard is a single-user app. It runs on Henry's own laptop and is only reachable from that laptop (and, optionally, from his own devices over a private network). It is not a public service, and nobody else can sign up for it or use it.

What it shows

  • The date, time and today's calendar events
  • University deadlines
  • An investment portfolio summary, read from a spreadsheet that the app creates in its owner's Google Drive
  • Later, unread email subjects from its owner's own inboxes

How it uses Google

The app signs in to its owner's Google account to read a holdings spreadsheet it created itself. It asks only for access to files it creates, so it cannot see any other Drive files. All access is read-only apart from setting up that one spreadsheet, and it never sends, changes or deletes email.

Privacy policy
Henry's Dashboard is a personal project and is not affiliated with Google.
